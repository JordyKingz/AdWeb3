## Current decisions

- Confirmed product direction: Go backend, Android first with iOS deferred, offline gameplay with online-only monetary eligibility, points/USDT pilot before any tradable platform token.
- Confirmed owned-game operating model: 30% players / 60% operations / 10% contingency from **earned net advertising revenue**. This split does **not** govern grant spending.
- SDK addition: a reusable one-game integration package, example, and data-flow documentation are proposed within the pilot. Free SDK access and a 20% commission are confirmed for the later external-publisher beta, leaving 80% to ordinary partner apps; full onboarding, publisher settlement, and additional platforms need separate scope and funding.
- Recommended grant scope: Avalanche C-Chain architecture, Fuji testnet demonstration using a clearly labeled test token, and a bounded Android adaptation of Candy Blocks as the game candidate, subject to game-scope and estimate validation.
- AdWeb3 has a researched [feature spec](../../docs/features/adweb3-platform/feature-spec.md), but no platform implementation. The mobile portfolio exists separately; it is not proof of a deployed advertising or reward system.
- Android is confirmed; Google Play reward-policy and launch-market assessment remain open. A testnet demonstration is the proposed funded outcome; production cash payouts and Google Play acceptance are not promised.

## Implementation planning

The [draft implementation plan](../../docs/features/adweb3-platform/implementation-plan.md) covers five Android/testnet pilot phases and two separately scoped publisher-beta phases. It includes test-first tasks, review gates, rollback notes, and evidence mappings. Its grant-milestone mapping shows that funding, delivery expiry, Android integration, and Fuji evidence span different engineering phases; the proposed grant timing/budget still needs Android-specific validation before submission. No implementation has started.
